CONTEXT: This illustration supports a LinkedIn business carousel for executives
and founders. Subject matter is business, work, productivity, strategy, and
tools — never personal life, family scenes, casual entertainment, vacation
imagery, food, sports, or unrelated objects.

Illustration in a polished dark editorial vector aesthetic.

Genre references: Stripe Press editorial illustrations, Apple keynote dark-mode
product slides, Linear / Vercel / Notion marketing illustrations on dark
backgrounds, B2B SaaS investor-deck dark themes, New York Times Opinion section
dark-mode digital illustrations. The mood is calm, considered, and expensive —
never moody, never grungy, never atmospheric-drama. The image reads as a
deliberate modern marketing asset.

Illustration vocabulary:
- A single focal subject rendered as a clean editorial vector. Prefer (in this
  order to counter the model's bias toward dashboard imagery): an abstract
  composition representing a concept (a node graph, a path, a connection, a
  stylized network), a stylized object representing a tool or workflow (a key,

- Subject is ALWAYS an object, scene, or abstract composition — NEVER a person.
  An anonymous hand or arm may interact with the focal object, but the face,
