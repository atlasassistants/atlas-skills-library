# midnight-editorial

**Best fit:** Executive / B2B SaaS audiences with a dark brand background.

**Visual feel:** Polished dark editorial vector. Single clean focal subject on a solid dark background. Accent color used as a focal highlight (a UI element, an underline, a status pill) — never as a glow or atmospheric effect.

**Strongest for:** Strategy frameworks, product launches, decisions, financial topics, and investor-facing content where dark brand themes signal seriousness without sliding into moody photo aesthetics.

**Weakest for:** Warm or aspirational content, narrative storytelling that needs human presence, light-bg brands.
