# pastel-diagram-marker

**Best fit:** Framework-led, data-heavy content.

**Visual feel:** Structured diagrams or single narrative metaphor scenes in soft pastel-marker treatment.

**Strongest for:** Frameworks, matrices, process flows, data breakdowns, step-by-step comparisons, and narrative metaphor scenes (e.g., a figure on a treadmill, a person at a fork in the road, a tower being built) when the source frames an idea around a concrete metaphor.

**Weakest for:** Dark or serious topics, editorial commentary, photojournalism subjects, minimalist corporate aesthetics.
