# documentary-noir

**Best fit:** Editorial-mood carousels with a dark brand background, where a moody photographic feel matches the topic. For most executive dark-bg use cases, prefer `midnight-editorial` instead.

**Visual feel:** Dark, high-contrast, grainy, photojournalism feel with strong negative space and a single small focal subject.

**Strongest for:** Provocative takes, industry critiques, sobering data, and topics where the moody photographic aesthetic reinforces the tone.

**Weakest for:** Upbeat or aspirational content, frameworks and how-tos, pastel-friendly audiences, anything that needs to feel warm or approachable, and most executive product / strategy / data carousels (use `midnight-editorial` for those).
