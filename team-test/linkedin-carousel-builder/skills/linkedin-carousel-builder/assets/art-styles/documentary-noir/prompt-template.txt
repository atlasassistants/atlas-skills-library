CONTEXT: This illustration supports a LinkedIn business carousel for executives
and founders. Subject matter is business, work, productivity, strategy, and

Illustration in a documentary-noir photographic aesthetic.

Genre references: Magnum Photos street photography, Sebastião Salgado's social
documentary work, Polaroid noir aesthetic, film-noir movie stills, black-and-
white photojournalism. The image reads as a single dramatic photograph captured
under harsh single-source light — never an illustration, never a diagram. The
mood is serious, evidentiary, weight-of-real-life.

Illustration vocabulary:
- A single dramatic subject under hard directional light: a hand reaching, a
  pair of hands working, a single object on a table (a book, a document, a
  tool, a desk item), a shadow on a wall, a folder or paper being held. The
  subject is ALWAYS an object — never a person.
- High-contrast monochrome rendering with deep blacks and luminous whites
- Visible film grain, slight scratches, and paper texture
- Strong negative space holding tension — the subject is small relative to the
  frame
- Background: {background_color}, treated as deep shadow or fog
- Color: {accent_name} ({accent_color}) is the ONLY color in an otherwise

Avoid:
- Multiple subjects competing for attention
- Bright or saturated palettes (this image is monochrome plus one accent)
- Flat-vector or marker treatments
- Cartoon or stylized characters
- Centered text labels treated as the subject
- **Photographs of people, portraits, faces, or human figures of any kind.**
  The subject must be an object (book, paper, folder, tool, desk item, shadow

  within photographs of people either — a book or document held in the frame
  must NOT show a portrait, headshot, or face on its cover or pages.
