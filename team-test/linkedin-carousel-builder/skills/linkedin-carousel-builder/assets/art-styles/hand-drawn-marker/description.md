# hand-drawn-marker

**Best fit:** Personal-brand executives, coaches, consultants.

**Visual feel:** Hand-drawn marker sketches, less polished, human, scribbled callouts.

**Strongest for:** Personal stories, life lessons, coaching frameworks, opinion takes, anything where authenticity matters more than polish.

**Weakest for:** Corporate or institutional audiences, technical diagrams requiring precision, finance or legal contexts where polish signals credibility.
